## App

- **Sign-in:** Google sign-in (email sign-in per the brief as secondary; Apple later).

- **Reporting:** push notification to Didi after each weekly run.

## Product context (from Didi, 2026-11-10)

- **Buyers and users:** parents are the primary buyers. They are anxious and want trust and peace of mind, and are reached through Facebook and WhatsApp. Learners are the users. They are tired and under pressure, respond to short video, humour and hope, and are reached through TikTok and Instagram. Teachers and schools are a bulk channel.
- **Market:** South Africa, mobile first, many users on limited data.
- **The book:** 52 weekly lessons (MLS 1 to 52), one for every week of the Grade 12 year, in English ("Winning Strategies for Grade 12") and Afrikaans ("Wenner Strategieë vir Graad 12").
- **Known lesson titles (examples, not the full list):** Momentum; Lessons from a Salmon (Lesse van 'n Salm); The Comfort Zone (Gemaksone); Entropy (Entropie); The Marshmallow Experiment (Marshmallow Eksperiment); Pressing the Self-Destruct Button; The Wait Is Almost Over (Die Wag Is Byna Verby); A Good Start to Project Matric.
- **Core IP:** the DCA formula, the DNA of a Successful Matriculant, the Pencil story, Force Field Analysis and five profiling questionnaires. Their content comes only from the book and is never invented.
- **Foreword:** Gerald Williamson, Clinical Psychologist and Educationist.
- **Website and accounts:** winstrat12.co.za (plain HTML, Gary's hosting) shares one Supabase login with the app. First account features: free account (unlocks free planners, lead capture), My Library (re-download books in both languages and bonus files by tier), parent linking both ways with weekly progress updates to parents.
- **Later:** school and teacher accounts with bulk licences.
- **Affiliate programme (planned):** Student Ambassadors 20 percent, Educator Partners 15 percent, Influencer Affiliates 25 percent commission.
- **Key differentiators:** the 52-week lesson journey (one lesson unlocks per week, progress, countdown to trials and finals) and saved self-assessments (the questionnaires online, with saved results that can be retaken).
- **Starting visual system:** the September prototype (ws12-dashboard.html, not in the repo). Deep navy #10163F, indigo-violet #3B33C9, gold #F9C22E, mint streak card, cream tip card. Used unless the reference image says otherwise.
- **Timing:** from November 2026 marketing targets the Class of 2027. NSC trial exams are around August to September; final exams October to November.
