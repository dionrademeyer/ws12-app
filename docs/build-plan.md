Companion to `build-brief.md` (the Master Build Prompt). Build the app in stages, not in one shot. The tracked version of the sprints, with acceptance criteria, is `SPRINTS.md`.

Book > Chapters > Strategies > Topics > Lessons > Activities > Quizzes > AI explanations > Study plans

## Version 1 in 7 sprints (plus Sprint 0)

- **Sprint 0 - Product Bible**
- **Sprint 1 - Foundation:** React/TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase, authentication, database, app shell, navigation
- **Sprint 2 - Student Identity:** registration, Google login, onboarding, profile, subjects, exam date, My Library, parent linking
- **Sprint 3 - Dashboard:** countdown, today's plan, tasks, streak, Tip of the Day, "Your Next Win"
- **Sprint 4 - Productivity:** goals, planner, study sessions, streak engine
- **Sprint 5 - Intelligence:** progress analytics, personal recommendations, learner insights
- **Sprint 6 - AI Tutor:** chat interface, AI service layer, knowledge-base architecture, first controlled WS12 content
- **Sprint 7 - Launch:** PWA, performance, security, analytics, testing, deployment, app-store preparation

## The WS12 Product Bible

The Constitution answers: Who are we? Why do we exist? What do we believe?

The Product Bible answers: Exactly what are we building? How does it work? Who is it for? What does every screen do?

It is the source of truth for Claude, future developers, AI agents, designers and the team, and is completed before serious production code is written.

Chapters:

1. Learner Persona
2. Complete App Architecture
3. Screen-by-Screen UX
4. Feature Priorities
5. User Journeys
6. Database Architecture
7. AI Agent Architecture
8. Melvin's Content to AI Knowledge Architecture
9. Monetisation
10. Analytics and KPIs
11. Admin Dashboard
12. School Portal
13. Parent Portal
14. Future App Store Strategy
